THEME 02

Bento Dashboard

Modern, data-driven and interactive

A command-centre feel with modular tiles, live-looking statistics and soft glowing colour. It puts DAO's results and programmes front and centre.

Blue · Crimson · Violet · Cyan
  • 3D card-stack hero with floating live statistics

Field Stories

Warm, human and editorial

A magazine-style design that leads with people and their stories. Warm paper tones, elegant headlines and big photography, in the spirit of major humanitarian publications.

Paper · Navy · Crimson
