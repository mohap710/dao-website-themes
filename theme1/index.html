01 — Mission

Deep action.
Lasting trace.

We exist to leave a deep and dignified mark on the lives of people affected by conflict and climate shocks — working alongside communities, never above them.

Zero tolerance for corruption.Every allocation is traceable from donor to doorstep.

A.

Our Mission

To deliver principled, accountable humanitarian and development action — shelter, protection, education, health and environmental resilience — to displaced people, returnees and the communities who host them.

B.

Our Vision

Safe, self-reliant communities where every person — regardless of origin, status or ability — lives with dignity and opportunity.

C.

Our Values

  • Zero tolerance for corruption
  • Radical transparency
  • Equality & non-discrimination
  • Inclusion by design
D.

Who we stand with

Equal access for every group — designed in, never bolted on.

  • Returnees
  • Host communities
  • Internally displaced people
  • Refugees
  • Nomadic groups
  • People with special needs

03 — Operational Pillars

Four pillars. One continuum of care.

Select a pillar to expand its field interventions.

Environmental
Preservation

  • Soil Stabilizer Bricks (SSB) — kiln-free construction that saves forests.
  • Fuel-efficient stoves to discourage over-consumption of charcoal.
  • Community environmental awareness campaigns.
  • Green livelihoods linked to restoration.

Photo: Lisa Marie Theck / Unsplash

Child
Protection

  • Child Friendly Spaces (CFS) in camps and host communities.
  • Psychosocial support (PSS) training for parents and teachers.
  • Advocacy to ban corporal punishment in schools and homes.
  • Family Tracing & Reunification (FTR) for separated children.

Photo: Salah Darwish / Unsplash

Civilian
Protection

  • Shielding refugees, IDPs and adolescent girls during firewood and water collection.
  • Gender-based violence (GBV) prevention and response.
  • PSEA referral networks with confidential reporting.
  • Group counselling for survivors and families.

Photo: Ayano Tosin / Unsplash

Education in
Emergencies

  • Temporary Learning Spaces (TLS) built within weeks of displacement.
  • Parent–Teacher Association (PTA) capacity building.
  • Quality learning and teaching supplies.
  • Inclusive classrooms for learners with special needs.

Photo: Emmanuel Ikwuegbu / Unsplash
