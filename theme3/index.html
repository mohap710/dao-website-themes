Our Mission

Feature · Why we exist

To leave a deep and dignified trace on every life touched by conflict.

Depth Action Organization was founded on a simple conviction: that aid is only as strong as the trust behind it. We work alongside returnees, host communities, nomadic populations, adolescent girls and people with special needs — not as recipients, but as partners in recovery. Every shelter raised, every child protected, every classroom opened is measured against one question: did it restore dignity?

Our programmes are designed with communities, delivered with accountability, and aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals — so that today's emergency response becomes tomorrow's lasting development.

Zero Tolerance for Corruption

Sectors of Action

Four focus areas, one continuum of care.

Select a sector to open its story.

Sector 01

Child Protection

Childhood, protected and restored — through safe spaces, reunited families and caregivers equipped to help children heal.

  • Child Friendly Spaces (CFS)
  • Family Tracing & Reunification (FTR)
  • Duty-bearer & caregiver PSS training
  • Advocacy against corporal punishment

Sector 02

Civilian Protection & GBV

Shielding refugees, IDPs and host communities — and making every journey for water and firewood a safe one.

  • Protection for refugees and IDPs
  • PSEA referral pathways
  • Group counselling for survivors
  • Safe firewood & water gathering routes

Sector 03

Environment Preservation

Building from the soil up — protecting forests while creating durable homes and green livelihoods.

  • Soil Stabilizer Bricks (SSB) for eco-friendly construction
  • Fuel-efficient stoves reducing charcoal use
  • Anti-deforestation awareness campaigns

Sector 04

Education in Emergencies

Learning that never stops — even when home does. Classrooms within weeks of displacement for out-of-school children.

  • Temporary Learning Spaces (TLS)
  • PTA capacity building
  • Quality learning supplies for out-of-school children
